## Learning alignment

Primary: identify inclusions from services/commissions/rent, separate source/type from treatment, distinguish cash received from income earned using evidence. Supporting: records must agree in scope, payment channel and summary coverage. Assessment is for an individual. Compensation is introduced through the demonstration; passive-income categories may appear as book vocabulary, but are not assessed without suitable records/rules. Corporate treatment is deferred; do not claim full coverage of the curriculum heading `Individuals and Corporations`.

The educational guide must distinguish `not income` from a statutory exclusion or income subject to a separate final tax. A genuine repayable borrowing is modeled as `Not income — repayable loan`, never as compensation, exempt business income, or an exclusion under Section 32(B). No debt cancellation or disguised payment is involved.
