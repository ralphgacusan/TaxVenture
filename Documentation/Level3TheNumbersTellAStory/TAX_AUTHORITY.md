## Applicability and assumptions

Bea is stipulated to be a resident Filipino professional, solely domestic active professional fees, graduated rates and itemized deductions already selected, non-VAT with supplied professional-fee declaration context. Revenue is gross of withholding. All services are earned and collected within the packet year; all admitted costs are paid/incurred, consumed within it, reasonable, wholly related to that work and supported. No compensation, final-tax/passive income, foreign income, prepayment, capital item, depreciation, loss, VAT/percentage-tax computation or tax-option optimization is assessed. Rent support includes paid invoice extracts and business-only use, not just a lease signature.

Marine Services is an ordinary domestic corporation with calendar-year accounts, operations and registration beginning **1 January 2024**, and no prior operation, predecessor MCIT, prior MCIT credit, NOLCO or incentive history. Relevant assets are **80,000,000 after excluding only qualifying premises land**. Its 6,000,000 net taxable income fails the small-company income ceiling despite satisfying the asset ceiling. It is not an RBE under EDR, SCIT, exempt entity or special-rate corporation. The family holiday is stipulated to be a private owner withdrawal, with no services, remuneration/fringe benefit, business travel or other compensatory basis; payroll/FBT/shareholder consequences are outside this computation. This is why the label 'administration' cannot make it deductible here.

Credits are actual supplied certificate amounts, applied once after tax due. No other quarterly payments, carryovers, foreign credits or refundable excess are in either packet. Bea's 250,000 income payment is a **subset of R2's 400,000 gross fees**, not new income. Marine's C-WHT is an annual certificate packet covering income already included in C-REV. Neither credit is an expense. Same-year restriction is this fixture's scope, not a universal prohibition on later credit use under 58(E).

Withholding provenance remains a specific content gate. Bea's 12,500 / 250,000 equals 5%; do not teach that as a universal rate. Marine's 300,000 / 8,000,000 equals 3.75%; **no 3.75% statutory transport-service withholding rate has been established**. Preserve the approved 300,000 as an assumed certified aggregate for development, not a rate-derived fact. Before graded release, the curriculum owner must supply/approve the itemized payer/ATC/period/income basis and eligibility for that aggregate, or explicitly approve its simplified supplied-credit treatment. A certificate-shaped prop alone cannot prove this. If the amount must change, update all expected balances, wrong fixtures and dialogue before adoption.

## Rounding contract for isolated development

Policy ID `L3-COMPUTATION-CENTS-v1`: source money has at most two decimal places; use invariant decimal strings/decimal arithmetic, never binary floating point. Keep exact precision in expressions and intermediate excess/marginal calculations. Round only the completed Tax Due to cents using half away from zero (`ROUND_HALF_UP` for these nonnegative cases), then subtract the exact cents credit and record Remaining Payable to cents. Attaching an intermediate tape does not round the stored value. Comparisons use the exact value at the declared output boundary, not a float tolerance. Negative/invalid/overflow expressions remain visible/check-needed, never silently clamped or turned into zero.

This is an explicit **curriculum policy proposal**, not a verified universal BIR computation rule. BIR whole-peso form-entry rounding in A2 is separately tested as a reference boundary. T01 does not implement filing or select a corporate filing form. An educator must approve cents versus whole-peso output and stage boundaries before graded release. All authored client figures are integral pesos, so either policy leaves their approved chains unchanged. Independent cent and peso midpoint fixtures are in [FIXTURE_CONTRACT](FIXTURE_CONTRACT.md).
