# TaxVenture campaign story — approved outline

Established: **6 November 2026, Asia/Manila**. Scope: the complete five-level narrative direction agreed in the planning conversation, including names, learning progression, continuing-case structure, choices and endings.

## Start here

Elijah helps individual Coast Tales taxpayers while gradually reviewing Bayview Property Services. Bayview appears to be an ordinary company; later records establish its connection to New Horizon Development Corporation. New Horizon uses misleading compliance claims to promote a waterfront redevelopment agreement that would displace local livelihoods. Supported evidence and earned Public Trust are both needed to resist the agreement.

This is an **approved story outline, not an implemented campaign or complete production specification**. The user explicitly approved the overall outline, then established the names and details below. Exact evidence packets, Level 4–5 interactions, financial fixtures and final trust balancing remain unfinished. Do not fill those gaps silently.

## Approved design commitments

1. One main client case closes in each of Levels 1–4. A single Bayview file continues from Level 2 and becomes Level 5's main assignment.
2. Apply the previous chapter's lesson to Bayview at the next chapter's opening; learn the next skill through the main client and side quest.
3. Bayview stages use familiar records, reference book, working fields and Auditor submission. They are short stages, not duplicate full chapters.
4. The Auditor retains interim Bayview work; no interim Client Copy/follow-through delivery. Main clients retain explanation and delivery.
5. An accepted stage is a success even while the overall case remains open. Do not label every correct stage Not Ready.
6. The corporation itself is the antagonist. Adrian represents knowing corporate pressure, not a lone employee who absolves the organization.
7. Names and branding initially appear unrelated. Reveal their relationship through records, not obvious matching logos.
8. Honest errors are correctable. Knowing commitments and their follow-through affect trust separately from first-attempt mastery.
9. Two main endings: community resistance succeeds, or the redevelopment agreement proceeds. Minor reactions may reflect professional history; they are not additional main endings.
10. Optional exploration gives recognition and context, never the sole evidence or a hidden victory requirement.
11. Winning rejects this harmful agreement and leaves room for fair community-led development. Development itself is not the antagonist.
12. Chapter cutscenes are intended only at the start and end. The latest user request fixes each new Level 1–3 scene at 15 seconds; the existing Level 1 intro remains its opening and is not shortened. The separate [four-ticket cutscene pack](../ChapterCutscenes/README.md) specifies five new scenes and a final wiring ticket. Ticket content is planned work, not implementation or rendered acceptance; Level 4–5 cinematics remain outside that pack.
