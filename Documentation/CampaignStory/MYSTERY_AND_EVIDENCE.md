# Mystery, clues and evidence boundaries

Part of the [approved campaign outline](README.md). This is the agreed reveal structure; exact evidence documents remain to be authored.

## 1. Intended player experience

Players first understand individual tasks, then notice recurring details, question their meaning, recognize a connection and finally prove what happened. Some players may guess early; the evidence-driven work should still satisfy them.

The player may be uncertain **why something matters**, but should know **what the current task is**. An incomplete background cannot omit facts needed to make a correct classification or computation. Do not manufacture suspense through unclear instructions or withholding obvious client identity.

Use concrete records and ordinary explanations before conclusions. Suspicion → relationship → deliberate act → responsibility are separate advances. Ownership does not prove intent; a payment does not by itself prove a bribe; matching stationery does not establish a scheme.

## 2. Knowledge and reveal ladder

| Level | What is established | What can be suspected | What remains unproven |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | New Horizon promotes Coast Tales Tomorrow; people live/work in the affected community. | Residents may be hopeful or uncertain about change. | Bayview affiliation, Desk funding, alterations, hidden displacement plans. |
| 2 | Bayview's ordinary identity/business; Rosa's omission and CoastCare preparer; optional survey reference. | Prepared work deserves checking; survey may affect vendors. | Deliberate sabotage, funding chain, corporate benefit, common authorization. |
| 3 | Bayview income discrepancy supported by originals; familiar CoastCare/preparer detail. | The cases may share a process or service connection. | Why the process failed; who ordered it; whether the issue was deliberate. |
| 4 opening | Selected company expense/payment needs support; computation reviewed within available scope. | Bayview's CoastCare relationship may matter. | Full purpose and instructions behind the connection. |
| 4 closing | CT-07 project identity; independent ownership connection; version evidence of a deliberate unsupported change. | Wider organized conduct is plausible. | Full scope, all responsible actors, complete benefit and redevelopment chain. |
| 5 | Supported records connect acts, instructions, authorization, funding, financial benefit and redevelopment representations. | Only remaining clearly labeled questions, if any. | Do not claim beyond the actual final evidence. |

The earlier idea of an income finding in Level 2 and expense finding in Level 3 was revised. Final order applies prior-level learning: classification L2, income finding L3, expenses/computation L4, return/integrated review L5.

## 3. Recurring clues

### CoastCare footer

Use the exact approved organization name and recognizable footer on appropriate prepared documents: **Prepared with CoastCare Tax Assistance**. Rosa names the service in conversation. Later company records repeat a meaningful connection.

The footer establishes preparer/service identity only. It does not prove that all work from the service is fraudulent, that the individual taxpayer intended misconduct, or that a specific worker authored the alteration.

### CT-07

- Level 2: Kanor's optional letter refers to a survey marked CT-07.
- Level 3: A supporting company record can repeat the reference as ordinary context. This secondary placement was approved as the clue concept; exact record remains undecided.
- Level 4: A project register explicitly maps CT-07 to Coast Tales Tomorrow. Independent ownership records make the Bayview/New Horizon connection reliable.
- Level 5: Instructions and history establish authorized actions and their relationship to the project.

Avoid making every document conspicuously repeat CT-07. Use a small number of purposeful placements. Case notes can recall the reference so recognition does not depend on memorization.

### Slogan and promise

“A better waterfront. A place for everyone.” appears naturally on polished signs and in Adrian's public introduction. More specific material/conversation promises affordable continued spaces for vendors. Internal plans later contradict that promise.

The losing ending can place familiar signs beside new fencing. The meaning changes because players know the consequences, not because a cutscene invents an earlier promise.

### Places and ambitions

Kanor's tools/shop, Rosa's business, Bea's studio ambition and public waterfront access provide emotional recognition. These are story anchors, not automatic evidence of financial manipulation. Let residents have mundane conversations before they become threatened people in an ending.

## 4. Optional Level 2 chest and letter

### Approved delivery

- Same chest style/mechanic used in Level 1, with a different placement/location and contents.
- Contains a copied letter from **Mang Kanor**, local repair-shop owner, to the property company about the waterfront survey.
- Mentions **CT-07** and asks whether existing vendors can remain at affordable rents/what happens to their spaces.
- Tone is routine but unresolved, not a secret warning announcing crime.
- Exact location, unlock, physical presentation, collection timing and reward are open.

Illustrative meaning, not final production copy:

> Please confirm whether existing vendors can stay at the same rent. We need to know before renewing our spaces.

The letter should make modest sense when found: someone wants clarity about their workspace. Later it gains a second meaning. Do not deliberately write nonsense so players think it is useless.

### Optionality and payoff

Found letter: retain it as a readable record and enable an extra comparison/acknowledgment when CT-07 is identified. A character/Elijah can recognize Kanor's earlier concern.

Missed letter: required Level 4–5 records independently establish the project identity, ownership and relevant conduct. Never gate a vital witness, sufficient evidence, final disclosure or winning ending solely on this chest.

No agreed trust/coin award exists for finding the letter. Exploration should not quietly become a morality or victory gate. Do not award invented bonuses.

## 5. Evidence design obligations

The following are **required proof questions for future design**, not fabricated existing documents:

| Final claim | What future evidence must establish | Insufficient alone |
|---|---|---|
| Bayview belongs to/is controlled by New Horizon | Reliable ownership/organizational relationship, entity identity and relevant period | Similar logo, rumor or shared address alone |
| CoastCare is affiliate-funded | Identified payer, recipient, period, purpose and supported affiliation chain | A vague payment label |
| A source was altered | Original, later version, specific difference and order/history | Two unexplained contradictory totals |
| Alteration was deliberate | An instruction/authorization or similarly reliable link to the change | Any arithmetic mistake |
| Corporation is responsible | Company authority/instructions and connection to actions; appropriate scope | Adrian being unpleasant or one rogue act |
| Company benefited financially | Supported treatment and effect in the authored corporate scenario | A larger or smaller tax figure without explanation |
| Local reports misrepresented reliability | Private report, supporting originals and unsupported claim/selection process | CoastCare footer alone |
| Project threatens existing livelihoods | Actual internal space/lease plans and contrast with public commitments | An ominous building picture |
| These acts support the redevelopment strategy | Documented project/instruction/benefit relationship | Coincidence across unrelated errors |

Provide reasonable alternatives to test. The player can revise an early interpretation without being punished for failing to guess the twist. Final supported findings must distinguish verified facts, inference and unresolved matters.

## 6. Client boundaries

- **Rosa:** Initial missing income and preparer connection are verified; deliberate targeting requires specific later evidence. Her omission alone is not proof of a plan to portray residents as noncompliant.
- **Bea:** Personal/duplicate costs and prepared-draft concern are limited case facts. Do not convert every source error into planted evidence. She stays primarily a personal client.
- **Nina/Marine Services:** Cooperative company, distinct from Bayview. Conflicting drafts teach version review; not automatic involvement in New Horizon's scheme.
- **Marco:** Authorized cooperation, not secret theft or omniscience. Exact records he can access require design.
- **Quax:** Good-faith endorsement/poor oversight, not a late unsupported secret-villain twist.

## 7. Case note and return handoff

Use compact retained context:

**Current stage:** Accepted / Needs correction / current work pending.

**Overall case:** Open — awaiting additional records or authorized review.

- **Confirmed:** What this stage actually established.
- **Still unclear:** One specific unanswered question.
- **Next record requested:** Why another stage is needed.

At next-level reception, provide a short recap and direct Elijah to the returned file. Notes should not present future conclusions before the player discovers them. Exact UI, save representation, labels beyond the agreed meanings and clerk lines require implementation design.

## 8. Fairness checks before finalizing packets

- Every required stage has enough reliable information for its assigned answer.
- A clear playable task remains available even when story significance is uncertain.
- Each level resolves a small investigative question rather than saying only come back later.
- Every decisive accusation has more support than a recurring symbol/reference.
- Optional clues enrich the recognition but do not create a hidden dead end.
- Required late records explain why they were not available earlier.
- Revisit/recap does not repeat rewards, restart completed local cases or erase player work.
- Final evidence makes earlier stages useful; Level 5 does not replace the mystery with an unrelated new problem.
