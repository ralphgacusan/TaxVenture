# Five-level story and gameplay flow

Part of the [approved campaign outline](README.md). This document describes the intended revised campaign, not a claim that every sequence already exists.

## 1. Learning rhythm and shared interactions

**Learn a concept through a main client → apply it independently to Bayview at the next level's opening → learn the next concept through the new main client.**

Levels 1–4 have a main client case that reaches a local resolution. Levels 2–4 also contain one short Bayview stage. Level 5 makes that same file the main assignment rather than introducing another unrelated final client.

Bayview is not a full duplicate case every chapter. Preserve familiar receive/read/reference/complete/review mechanics but keep each stage narrow. The user's final direction is **short**, without a fixed duration; the earlier suggested 3–5-minute target was not adopted as a measured requirement.

### Bayview stage route, Levels 2–4

1. Receptionist announces the assignment or returning file; additional records are on the desk.
2. Elijah receives/opens the familiar case folder.
3. Read supplied records; consult relevant Tax Code Book/reference material and use the existing appropriate value-transfer interactions.
4. Complete only the current supported scope: identity/classification, income findings or computation, depending on level.
5. Submit to the Auditor through the familiar review flow.
6. If incorrect or unsupported, receive comments and correct the work. Preserve useful progress rather than restarting all stages.
7. Auditor accepts the supported stage and retains the file for later records/review.
8. Main client assignment begins. No interim corporate Client Copy is handed to Elijah for a client-delivery errand.

**Status:** Current stage = Accepted; Overall case = Open / awaiting additional records. Use Needs correction for real errors. A correct result must not repeatedly read Not Ready as if the player failed.

### Main client route

Meet/receive assignment → relevant side quest and new learning → office application → Auditor review and corrections → explain supported result → actual client-copy handover → chapter resolution.

The side quest provides understanding and supported inputs; it should not silently solve the main office case. Lessons, source selection and document reading remain legible on mobile. Retain physical paper, book, folder, annotation, calculator and stamp vocabulary rather than introducing unrelated interfaces for each chapter.

## 2. Level 1 — Tutorial Level / Meet the People

“Tutorial Level” is an existing catalog label. “Meet the People” is a descriptive story heading, not a separately approved final catalog rename.

### Focus and cast

- **Main client:** Marie Bautista, salaried BPO employee.
- **Required profile cast:** Marie, Jhun-jhun, Milo and Zorbix.
- **Support:** Arthur, receptionist, Auditor; existing optional town cast plus proposed Mang Kanor introduction.
- **Side quest:** Know Your Taxpayer.
- **Learning:** Citizenship/residence distinctions, taxpayer classification, taxpayer type and income source; connecting conclusions to client facts and records.
- **Not the chapter's new learning:** Independent tax computation. That belongs to Level 3.

### Intended flow, preserving current concepts

1. Campaign intro introduces Coast Tales/Elijah and hands over to the playable setting. Existing exact intro remains separate from future cutscene revisions.
2. Learn movement, looking, Notes and Supply Counter access. Purchases remain optional.
3. Meet Arthur and inspect his reviewed former-client file. The source client is not one of the four profile taxpayers; the example supports learning without revealing Marie's answers.
4. Meet the four profile taxpayers in any order and collect their profiles. Dialogue describes lived circumstances, not answer labels.
5. Return to the board/Arthur follow-up. Independently classify profiles, submit/check and correct misses.
6. Begin Marie's active office case. Use her client information and supporting ID/employment/background/2316 records as appropriate to current scope. Conversations connect her familiar story to the case folder.
7. Establish taxpayer classification, type and income source with reference support; submit to the Auditor and correct when needed.
8. After approval, obtain Marie's client copy, explain the result in the conference room and hand it over. Complete the wrap-up and return to Career.

### Narrative function

Elijah earns an initial place in the community through actual service. Marie is met as a person before becoming paperwork. Board feedback and review establish that mistakes are part of learning, while client delivery establishes accountability beyond approval.

New Horizon is present through ordinary signs and NPC mentions: improvements, jobs and affordable vendor spaces. Residents may reasonably welcome the proposal. Adrian can have a brief public introduction consistent with that tone; exact staging is unresolved. Introduce Kanor and his repair livelihood briefly. Do not reveal ownership, deliberate alteration or secret instructions.

### Exit state

Marie helped and delivered; classification foundations learned; residents and threatened places established; no Bayview file assigned yet. The next level provides an independent application of those foundations.

## 3. Level 2 — Something Does Not Match

### A. Opening Bayview stage: identify the taxpayer

Receptionist introduces Bayview Property Services, Inc. as a routine staged review requested through male bookkeeper Marco Ramos. Supplied identity/background is clear enough to do the work. The company appears to maintain and lease property.

Apply Level 1: establish entity identity, taxpayer category, business activity and income sources. A short corporate reference bridges the difference from Marie's individual case. Do not pretend that an individual classification lesson alone supplied every corporate rule. No corporate tax calculation yet.

Submit the limited stage. Auditor comments, accepts supported work and retains the file pending further records. The player should understand that this is accomplishment, not a failed unresolved case.

### B. Main case: Rosa

- **Client:** Aling Rosa, Rosa's Laundry.
- **Side quest:** Mark the Money!
- **Learning:** Income recognition, differentiating loans/other receipts from income, comparing sources, reconciliation, supported findings and readiness.

1. Receive Rosa's prepared summary at the office and identify that support is incomplete.
2. Record a justified preliminary hold rather than treating an unverified summary as complete.
3. Visit Rosa's stall near the plaza and obtain records.
4. Use the annotation toolkit to inspect and mark four records. Records cover ordinary laundry income, spare-washing-machine rental, a separate blanket-washing job and a loan deposit. The paid blanket-washing job is absent from the initial summary.
5. Rosa transitions into the office consultation route; maintain one character rather than duplicate actors.
6. After marking the records and before the first complete check, make the explicit Rosa commitment choice. Show the unresolved concern first; unsupported reassurance requires clear separate confirmation. See [trust rules](CHOICES_TRUST_AND_ENDINGS.md).
7. Reconcile the summary against evidence, make a supported assessment and submit to Auditor review. All branches require corrections and approval.
8. Explain the outcome and deliver Rosa's client copy through the normal physical follow-through. Complete the chapter and retain the actual choice history.

### C. Narrative discoveries and optional letter

Rosa confirms CoastCare prepared the original summary. At this point, discrepancy plus preparer identity does not prove intentional sabotage. Do not reopen her completed case later merely to manufacture additional gameplay or an accusation.

An optional chest, using the same chest style/mechanic as Level 1 with different placement and contents, contains Kanor's copied letter. It asks about CT-07 surveys and affordable continued occupancy. It does not award mandatory evidence, reveal the plot or determine the ending. Precise location, interaction and any reward are not finalized.

### Exit state

Bayview classification stage accepted; Rosa's records reconciled and copy delivered; CoastCare known; trust consequences/commitment history retained; optional letter remembered if found. Level 3 applies the newly learned income-verification skills.

## 4. Level 3 — The Numbers Tell a Story

### A. Opening Bayview stage: income review

Receptionist announces that more Bayview records have arrived and the file is on the desk. A short recap uses the retained case note rather than repeating all prior conversations.

Apply Level 2: compare an income summary with originals, reconcile a discrepancy and record a supported finding. A familiar CoastCare/preparer reference suggests a connection. The discovery is a reason to ask a question, not sufficient evidence of corporate manipulation.

Submit to the Auditor, correct if needed, then accepted file remains with the office. Exact income discrepancy and financial figures require future authorship.

### B. Main case: Bea

- **Client:** Beatriz Mendoza, freelance logo/menu illustrator saving toward a studio.
- **Side quest:** Worth the Tax?
- **Learning:** Business purpose, allowable supported costs, personal and duplicate exclusions, taxable income, graduated computation and withholding credits.
- **Active main-client scope:** Bea only. No required Nina/company appointment in this level under the revised plan.

1. Receive Bea's appointment; earlier optional acquaintance is acknowledged without being required.
2. Meet Bea outside, inspect her envelope/records and begin the compact paper-machine activity.
3. Read one record, feed it halfway, select Income / Deduct / Leave Out, supply the required reason where relevant and lever-file. Check small batches and correct before progressing.
4. Supported categories include two earning entries, three business cost entries, a personal birthday purchase excluded for personal purpose and a printing record excluded because it is already represented.
5. Collect the corrected small Income & Expense Summary into Bag/case support. This is the office input, not a second unrelated worksheet.
6. At the office, use the familiar folder and a short first-use calculator lesson if not already taught.
7. Build three source-supported stages: income less deductions; tax due under the selected reference; remaining payable after withholding credit. Do not count a certificate as new income or a business cost.
8. Submit the current computation to the Auditor, correct unsupported steps and obtain approval for computation review.
9. Obtain Bea's computation copy, explain the credit/remaining balance and hand it over. Computation approval is not official filing or payment.
10. Record the limited Bea-supported concern from the prepared draft and receive Quax's message through the existing coordination role. Close the chapter without alleging intent or a broad scheme based solely on Bea.

### C. Existing financial fixture context

The documented Bea fixture has income 900,000, supported business costs 300,000, taxable income 600,000, tax due 62,500, withholding credit 12,500 and remaining payable 50,000. These are existing fictional source values, not a new tax-law verification, universal rule or permanently displayed answer key. Refer to the active content/fixture documents before implementation.

Bea's story stays personal. Her draft concern does not make all receipts planted or make her a proven deliberate target. Public Trust grows through supported service and delivery, not every calculation keystroke.

### Exit state

Income review completed on Bayview; CoastCare connection remains suggestive; Bea understands her computation and receives her copy; studio ambition has emotional weight. Level 4 applies her deduction/computation lessons.

## 5. Level 4 — Follow the Paper Trail

### A. Opening Bayview stage: expenses and computation

Additional records return through reception. Evaluate selected company expenses and construct a supported computation using Level 3's method with an appropriately supplied corporate reference. A payment connected to CoastCare has unclear purpose/support. Do not give away its full significance before the tracing activity.

Submit to the Auditor. Accepted current work is retained pending clarification and return-preparation support. Exact amount, deduction treatment, corporate rule context and document contents remain to be designed.

### B. Main case: Nina / Marine Services

- **Client representative:** Nina Cruz.
- **Company:** Coast Tales Marine Services, Inc., cooperative local transport-services business.
- **Side quest:** Paper Trail.
- **Learning:** Appropriate return/form selection, period confirmation, mapping verified figures, support assembly, draft/version comparison and simulated filing preparation.

Nina arrives with verified computation and conflicting draft-return documents. Teach return preparation rather than repeating Bea's whole receipt sorting/calculation puzzle. The Paper Trail activity establishes how to compare originals, identify version changes and determine supported entries. Its exact objects, puzzle steps and answer checks are unfinished.

Use the established office language and review/correction flow. Complete the chapter's main-client explanation and copy delivery. Keep the distinction between a prepared/reviewed draft, simulated filing and actual payment clear. Exact final output/receipt labels need Level 4 design.

Nina's company is not Bayview and not the antagonist. It is a positive corporate client whose cooperation contrasts with New Horizon's conduct.

### C. Brief closing return to the continuing case

1. New Bayview records become available through the legitimate review.
2. Apply the version-tracing method just learned from Nina's activity.
3. A project register identifies CT-07 as Coast Tales Tomorrow. Independent ownership records establish Bayview's New Horizon connection.
4. Version history supports a deliberate unsupported change. Exact instruction, actor and scope are not automatically proven by ownership alone.
5. If Kanor's letter was found, provide a recognition/comparison moment. If not, required records make the same investigation possible.
6. Adrian pressures Elijah to accept a replacement without resolving its conflict, promising speed and assistance for local businesses. The player knowingly accepts or checks an additional source.
7. Preserve the submitted work, chosen commitment and unresolved questions. Arthur/Auditor escalate to an authorized review for Level 5.

This closing return is an approved exception to the normal opening-only stage rhythm. It is a discovery and decision beat, not a second full corporate review duplicating the opening.

### Exit state

Nina helped; return/version skills learned; Bayview ownership link established; pressure choice recorded; wider investigation justified. The identity reveal is not yet complete proof of every action and motive.

## 6. Level 5 — Catch Me If You Comply

### Opening and focus

Bayview becomes the main assignment. Apply Level 4's return-preparation and support/version skills to its draft. Unresolved questions and authorization support a wider review; do not send Elijah to steal restricted records.

Marco's cooperation is evidence-driven. He supplies available records through authorized channels and does not suddenly know the entire scheme. Arthur and the Auditor retain professional roles.

### Main activity

**Catch Me If You Comply** remains the chapter's central investigative activity/side-quest concept. It is connected to completing the corporate case, not a separate income-sorting puzzle. The user explicitly retained this name rather than replacing Level 5 with another Paper Trail activity.

Use the broader explore → question → verify → substantiate rhythm already envisioned for the final chapter. Exact interactive steps and evidence-board presentation are not approved production mechanics yet.

### Intended investigation outputs

- Supported corporate identity, ownership, income, costs, computation and return treatment.
- Retained originals and altered summaries, with source/version relationships.
- Evidence of instructions/approval, who authorized changes and the limits of each finding.
- Affiliate funding and CoastCare relationship.
- Support for the financial benefit and the use of misleading reports in redevelopment persuasion.
- Contradiction between affordable-space promises and internal redevelopment/lease plans.

The challenge is connecting proven facts and establishing organizational responsibility. A surprising number alone is not enough. Honest errors in unrelated work remain possible.

### Final sequence

1. Resolve unsupported work through review/correction.
2. Complete the evidence-supported authorized finding.
3. Provide one limited opportunity to acknowledge and repair an earlier harmful commitment through explanation and concrete follow-through. Exact timing relative to the meeting is unfinished.
4. Adrian offers to preserve some assistance if the decisive corporate connection is softened/omitted.
5. Player chooses complete supported disclosure or knowing omission. Polite, calm full disclosure is a valid winning approach.
6. Present findings within the pending redevelopment process; community support reflects actual history.
7. Resolve to one of two main endings and acknowledge familiar clients/residents.

No unrelated fresh final case should displace the file the player has built since Level 2. The decisive new evidence must pay off earlier work rather than make it irrelevant.

## 7. Presentation and cutscene boundaries

The user selected start and end scenes only, roughly 10–15 seconds each, with basic animation, purposeful camera movement and little dependence on facial acting. Use existing characters, environments, compatible animation and simple prop movement where possible.

Openings frame the concern and clear first action; endings acknowledge what the player achieved and create a specific next question. Keep discoveries, comparisons, submissions and deliveries playable. Do not reveal the office answer in a cutscene or repeat the same explanation in scene, tutorial and dialogue.

Detailed scripts, subtitle budgets, shot timing and treatment of the longer existing campaign intro are deferred. Earlier sample Marie/Arthur lines were illustrative, not canonical dialogue.
