# World, organizations and characters

Part of the [approved campaign outline](README.md). All new organizations and supporting names are fictional story choices; name uniqueness has not been researched.

## 1. Premise and tone

Coast Tales is a coastal community of employees, freelancers, shopkeepers, service businesses and visitors. Its office, plaza, market and waterfront are places the player gets to know through actual work and ordinary conversation. It should feel worth protecting before the threat is explicit.

Elijah arrives as a new tax consultant. Arthur is the senior consultant and guide. Elijah's growing competence changes the scale of work entrusted to him: understand people, verify income, evaluate costs and compute tax, prepare returns, then substantiate an integrated corporate finding.

The campaign is a community-centered income-tax adventure with a gradually unfolding corporate mystery. Everyday client difficulties remain genuine, not merely disguises for a conspiracy. The mystery adds purpose to learning rather than replacing tax gameplay with unrelated detective work.

Keep the corporation's harm understandable and emotionally consequential. It need not announce evil intentions or threaten everyone overtly. Respectful language, useful-looking services and attractive promises can conceal damaging decisions.

## 2. New Horizon Development Corporation

- **Canonical full name:** New Horizon Development Corporation.
- **Common name:** New Horizon.
- **Role:** Main antagonist organization, seeking inexpensive control of valuable waterfront and market property.
- **Public project:** Coast Tales Tomorrow.
- **Slogan:** “A better waterfront. A place for everyone.”
- **Public promise:** Better facilities, jobs and affordable spaces where existing vendors can remain.
- **Actual direction:** Expensive commercial development, unaffordable replacement leases and displacement of local businesses; public waterfront access is threatened.
- **Visual identity:** Navy and gold, rising-sun mark, polished project signs.
- **Representative:** Adrian Reyes.

The corporation seeks a redevelopment agreement, not literal private ownership of every person or government function in town. Tax discrepancies do not automatically convey property or cancel a project. The company uses compliance claims to influence a pending approval and obtain local participation on favorable terms. Exact approval procedure remains to be designed within the fictional setting.

### Agreed scheme: two connected kinds of wrongdoing

1. **Its own financial reporting:** Unsupported income/expense treatment benefits the company. Bayview's staged review provides the player a natural route into checking the relevant figures. Exact transactions and amounts are not yet authored.
2. **Manufactured rescue:** Through its affiliate-funded CoastCare service, selected summaries are altered or mishandled and misleading unresolved-compliance reports are produced. The company uses apparent local unreliability to argue for intervention and offers assistance tied to restrictive redevelopment/relocation terms.
3. **False protection:** Residents are promised an affordable place in the improved district, while internal plans support costly replacement development instead.

The final evidence must connect the financial records, instructions, authorization and redevelopment benefit. The outline approves the scheme; it does not itself supply documentary proof or establish every early case as deliberate sabotage.

## 3. Bayview Property Services, Inc.

- **Canonical full name:** Bayview Property Services, Inc.
- **Common name:** Bayview.
- **Apparent business:** Ordinary property maintenance and leasing.
- **Role:** Continuing corporate client file from Level 2 to Level 5.
- **Contact:** Marco Ramos, bookkeeper.
- **Visual identity:** Muted blue-gray stationery and a plain building mark.
- **Hidden relationship:** Corporate ownership connects Bayview to New Horizon; confirmed in Level 4 through appropriate records.

Bayview is a real identified client, not an anonymous mystery packet. The player receives sufficient reliable context to do each assignment. What is initially unknown is the wider ownership relationship and purpose of questionable changes.

Marco requests a routine staged review through the office. Partial record delivery and the limited stage assignment explain why the file remains open. The office is not secretly investigating from the start. Arthur and the Auditor help escalate after evidence justifies an authorized review.

Do not invent the precise legal ownership chain, entity-registration facts, signatories, financial period or document identifiers beyond approved CT-07. Those require the evidence/fixture design pass.

## 4. CoastCare Tax Assistance

- **Canonical name:** CoastCare Tax Assistance.
- **Common conversational name:** CoastCare.
- **Role:** Apparently helpful preparation service and recurring lead across records.
- **Visual identity:** Teal and cream, sheltered-wave mark, approachable forms.
- **Recognizable footer:** “Prepared with CoastCare Tax Assistance”.
- **Funding relationship:** Secretly funded by New Horizon through an affiliate; documentary confirmation comes later.

CoastCare retains genuine originals but issues selected altered summaries and misleading compliance reports. Its exact staffing and instruction chain are not yet established. Some workers can be innocent; do not declare every employee complicit.

It is not a government tax authority. It cannot issue official tax assessments or make authoritative legal findings that businesses are noncompliant. Its private reports and the corporation's use of those reports must be presented as such.

Quax endorsed the service in good faith. That creates a reason for concern and acknowledgment of failed oversight without making him a hidden villain.

## 5. Coast Tales Tomorrow and CT-07

Coast Tales Tomorrow is the public-facing redevelopment proposal. **CT-07** is its recurring project reference. Early use resembles ordinary survey/property administration. Later a project register identifies the reference, while independent ownership records establish the corporate connection.

CT-07 should prompt a light-bulb moment, not function as a password or proof of intent. Characters and retained notes can recall it; player memory is not an ending gate.

## 7. Clients and community

### Marie Bautista

Local salaried BPO employee, born and raised locally and never resident abroad. Her chapter teaches individual circumstances and classification, not an unexplained financial conspiracy. The plaza conversation continues into her office case and delivered copy. She anchors the first successful client relationship and appears in the final community response without inventing an unsupported business loss for her.

### Aling Rosa

Runs Rosa's Laundry. Her prepared summary omits a separate blanket-washing payment. Records distinguish ordinary laundry receipts, washing-machine rental, the separate paid job and a loan deposit. The stalled review and Elijah's explicit reassurance/refusal choice establish professional trust. Her waterfront trading space becomes vulnerable under redevelopment; do not automatically imply her entire business is destroyed on approval.

### Beatriz “Bea” Mendoza

Freelance graphic artist designing logos and menus for local businesses. Keeps payments in notes and receipts, and saves toward a studio. Optional Level 1 conversations make her a familiar person, but Level 3 introduction works independently. Her chapter teaches supported costs and calculation. Her prepared draft provides a limited concern; do not declare her personal and duplicate receipts planted. Redevelopment threatens affordable opportunities for her studio.

### Nina Cruz and Coast Tales Marine Services, Inc.

Nina is the bookkeeper for a cooperative local transport-services company supplying island shops. This is a legitimate company distinct from Bayview and New Horizon. The revised campaign moves her deferred Level 3 appointment to Level 4 as the main return-preparation client. She supplies a verified computation and conflicting draft-return versions. Her cooperation teaches tracing and supports the broader inquiry without turning her employer into the antagonist.

### Mang Kanor

Male local shopkeeper running **Kanor's Repair Shop**, a small waterfront business repairing household fans, rice cookers and similar appliances. Affordable workspace and nearby regular customers sustain his livelihood. Establish him briefly in Level 1 before the optional Level 2 letter. His copied correspondence asks whether CT-07 surveys affect leases and whether vendors can afford to stay. The losing ending can show a relocation notice and packed tools.

### Other existing residents

- **Jhun-jhun:** Filipino engineer living and working in Qatar; visits home briefly. Level 1 profile illustrates differing citizenship and residence.
- **Milo:** Foreign-born restaurant owner settled locally with home and family. Level 1 profile, distinct from Kanor's business.
- **Zorbix:** Alien visitor on a paid 27-day project with return travel booked. Preserve the game's light fictional tone and classification role.
- **Aling Lorna:** Existing sari-sari owner with receipt/ledger conversations. Remains in town; she is not the letter author and does not need an invented waterfront stall.
- **Mang Dante:** Existing security guard, reserved and attentive to records.
- **Carlo:** Delivery rider and Bea's cousin, discussing costs and record keeping.
- **Mang Ernesto and Tess:** Existing supporting Level 2 town life; detailed new conspiracy roles not agreed.

The final outcome should acknowledge familiar residents and completed clients, but not fabricate identical losses for every character.

## 8. Relationships to preserve

New Horizon → corporate control of Bayview → affiliate-funded CoastCare is the broad concealed relationship. The exact funding chain must be authored and evidenced; do not assume an identical brand proves affiliation.

Elijah → Arthur/Auditor = learning and professional review. Elijah → main clients = explanation and completed service. Elijah → Marco = routine appointment that grows into authorized cooperation. Elijah → Adrian = public courtesy developing into pressure. Quax → CoastCare = initial endorsement followed by accountability.

Public trust is earned with residents, not purchased from the corporation. Good corporate clients such as Marine Services remain possible. The theme is responsible work and community agency, not that all businesses or all redevelopment are evil.
