# Tutorial Hub: known issues and risks

None of these have been seen in Play Mode yet; they are the places most likely to need a tweak.

1. **Office spawn point is computed, not placed.** Office drills stand the player 45% of the way from the reception doors' centre to the receptionist, on the floor found by a downward raycast. If that lands badly (in furniture, outside), change `spawn` / `face` in the case drill JSON (any scene path works), or adjust the 0.45 in `PracticeModeRuntime.PlacePlayer`.
2. **Hidden scene parts (`hide`).** Plaza drills switch off the office rooms (except the reception, which holds the door gate) and `Inside/MiscProps`; office drills switch off `Env/Vegetation`, `Env/Rocks`, `Env/Props`, `Env/Buildings` and the plaza NPCs. In an office drill the player can still walk outside, into a bare town. If a system misbehaves with a part switched off, remove that path from the drill's `hide` list.
3. **Practice case swap.** Case drills replace the loaded case with `case_002` right after the level starts (`CaseManager.LoadCase`). Anything that cached Marie's case before that (for example a conference-room client) still shows Marie. The case finale and consequence reactions after approval may refer to Marie; the result card covers them.
4. **Marco's answers.** `case_002` expects Self-Employed / Professional Income. The Tax Code Book also offers "Professional" as a taxpayer type, so the case may feel ambiguous. That is case content (the user's data), not hub code.
5. **The binder pose is computed, not placed (2026-10-17, untested).** The camera goes to the eyes of the `npc_` character at `SM_Prop_Desk_03` (eye height 92% of his height) and the binder rests just past the desk's front edge, above the desk, facing him; his renderers are switched off while we look through his eyes. Expected: a binder about a fifth of the desk wide, viewed about 50 degrees down. Tune with the serialized fields on `TrainingBinder3D` (`eyeHeight`, `binderReach`, `binderLift`, `characterPrefix`; add the component to an object in the 3D Menu yourself to edit them in the Inspector, and the bootstrap then skips adding another) or the defaults at the top of the file.
6. **The binder hides the menu buttons by name** (`play_button`, `tutorial_button`, `settings_button`, `sidequest_button`), like the settings board. A renamed button would stay visible behind the binder.
7. **Mastery "Your Screen" last goal** (`near:4:waypoint`) needs the objective guide to have a goal (a plaza client). If the arrows are turned off in guide.json, that goal cannot finish.
