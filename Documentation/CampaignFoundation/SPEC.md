# Campaign foundation specification

Status: Planned; source inspected, no implementation. Date: 2026-10-21.

## Intent and release boundary

Turn the current game into the first playable slice of the approved five-level income-tax adventure: Elijah earns public trust, completes corrected work, collects useful records, receives office-budget coins and improves his office. Mayor Quax replaces Arthur as guide and is physically present for most first-time demonstrations. Reuse existing movement, paper/drag/tap interactions, office/plaza, case validation, audio/settings, career board and practice binder.

Delivery A (T01-T17): shared foundation, main menu and Level 1 fully integrated. Delivery B (T18-T21): existing Rosa-only Level 2 integrated, including its deliberate ethical choice. Future chapters use the same records; do not implement their cases, computer benefits, evidence board, meeting or ending scenes now.

New story flow: New Game -> opening -> personally approach Quax -> meet four taxpayers -> collect profiles -> independent classification check and corrections -> Marie's guided office case -> client follow-through -> shop introduction -> chapter wrap-up -> career. Optional keepsake/supply exploration and saved sessions fit around required work. Shop access is optional; making a purchase never gates completion. Trust does not gate corrected learning progression.

## Observed baseline (2026-10-21)

- PlayerProgressData version 4 has mastery EXP/heart totals, best level records, badges, tutorial/practice records, storyFacts, intro-seen and one active Level2 checkpoint. totalReputation is ACCURACY-SCORE hearts, not social trust.
- PlayerProgressManager/SaveFileStore own one signed atomic save and backup. Signature key and SignedSinceVersion=3 are compatibility contracts. Some current mutations happen before Save returns; new campaign transactions must not inherit success-on-write-failure behavior.
- LevelScoring.Passed requires EXP AND score hearts at 80%; CareerProgress and IsLevelUnlocked rely on previous PASSED. Replace active story unlock semantics, not simply labels. Old player results are not imported into the fresh campaign.
- MenuEntry routes Play through the opening until first L1 finish, then the career board. Explicit New Game/Continue are absent. Settings has reset/replay paths that must not bypass new campaign rules.
- L1 profile lists, board placements/attempts and objective progress are runtime only; no equivalent L1 checkpoint exists. The board currently reveals correctness and penalizes wrong drops immediately.
- Level2Checkpointer already saves Rosa review phases, annotated records, frozen grade, findings, stamps, audit/clock and restoration. Level2 has physical Ready/Not Ready outcomes but fixed-order dialogue, not a knowing-choice conversation system.
- No general wallet/shop/inventory exists. Physical profile/folder/annotated-paper viewers exist and should be reused. Quax-named art was not found; inspect assets at implementation time and track art honestly.

## Authoritative campaign contracts

1. Separate mastery (XP/badges/best results), public trust (0..100 social confidence), and economy/ownership. Do not use legacy hearts as trust or add a second mastery heart bar. UI hearts exclusively represent public trust in the new campaign.
2. Active chapter completion requires the required quest corrected, case actually approved after correction and required finale/follow-through completed. XP, badge tier, public trust, optional pickups and purchases cannot block the next supported chapter. A mastery threshold may be descriptive, never a disguised story gate.
3. Story, mastery replay, practice and temporary direct-Editor runs have explicit modes. Campaign mutation commands refuse non-story mode. Replays may improve eligible best XP/badges, not coins/trust/choices/ownership/collectibles. Sandbox supplies are temporary and do not consume live inventory.
4. The first COMPLETE independent check freezes grading; drafts and compulsory sample demonstrations are unscored. L1 changes to four draft placements; the full board checks itself (user decision 2026-10-24, replacing a Check Board action). No correctness hint leaks before the first check. Corrections finish learning without improving the frozen tier. Gold=4/4, Silver=3/4, Bronze=0..2/4 after corrections. These are approved-pack implementation defaults. L2 retains its 12-decision frozen check and existing tier boundaries.
5. First story activity completion awards fixed coins once, even with a low first grade after correction. First chapter story history remains fixed; replay cannot rewrite it. Once committed, a choice or reward cannot be undone by exiting/reloading.
6. Ordinary mistakes affect mastery. Public-trust changes come from authored service/follow-through or explicit commitments/knowing decisions, not wrong-drop counts, wrong field types or automatic deadline misses. L1 has no intentional misconduct choice. T19 introduces the first in L2.
7. HUD/guide/presenters subscribe to snapshots/results; they never mutate campaign state from Update, display, animation completion or tutorial advancement. Existing reward notifications documented as presentation-only remain presentation-only.

L1 quest XP uses the current sanitized quest EXP/expPerWrong configuration against the frozen count of incorrect profiles, once per profile; corrections do not deduct again. With the inspected 50 EXP and 5 per wrong default, the first-check range is 30..50. Keep the current sanitized case XP/audit/late calculations and caps unless explicitly changed by a ticket; their legacy heart deductions are not social-trust changes. Badge tier depends on frozen accuracy, not the forgiving XP total or on-time restoration.

## Save and command boundary

Evolve PlayerProgressData/PlayerProgressManager, not a second progress singleton or PlayerPrefs economy. Next schema version is 5; preserve SignedSinceVersion=3 and signing key. Add a campaign record with campaignId, story position/run mode, trust, wallet, activity claims, item acquisitions, owned upgrade IDs, supplies/drawer quantities, display placements, commitments, choices/consequences, first completed chapter records and the active checkpoint. Retain exact original/working document IDs and revisions; never persist scene references or answer-key fields in player-facing item snapshots. Normalize repairs data deterministically and performs no file I/O.

Central commands accept stable scoped action IDs and return success/no-change/failure plus presentation facts. Acquisition, reward, purchase and consume/extend commands validate a candidate state, persist all related changes together, then publish the committed snapshot. On failure, retain previous memory/durable state, emit no success effect and allow a safe retry. Unique claims and arithmetic guards prevent double taps, repeated events, overflow and negative balances. Gameplay checkpoint and associated ownership/reward mutation must describe the same committed milestone.

Completion-claim identity is campaign + level + activity + reward kind, never transient run ID, animation or scene instance. Restarting an unfinished attempt cannot repay an earned quest. Pickups are campaign + collectible ID. Voucher use is campaign + level/case identity, shared across day rollover. A consumable purchase uses a fresh confirmed request ID per legitimate purchase and reuses that same ID for retry; a fixed product ID alone must not prohibit buying a second voucher. Nonconsumable ownership independently blocks duplicate purchase.

Use the existing atomic signed envelope for the NEW campaign. There is no old-progress import, conversion, mastery archive, archive screen or special pre-update backup. Initial values are XP0, no earned badges or completed chapters, trust40/100, coins0, no collected records/keepsakes or purchased upgrades, no supplies or story choices, and L1 available; essential starter tools remain available. Keep these defaults in one factory/configuration path, used by first New Game and later confirmed resets.

An existing pre-campaign save is not a valid Continue source. Detect incompatible data before the current normalization/old-score path can present it as an updated campaign; show 'Start the updated story' and offer New Game. Confirmed New Game uses the existing reset/storage owner to replace primary and normal backup generations, so recovery cannot resurrect discarded old progress. Cancel or a failed replacement does not publish a successful new campaign. Do not build or test historical schema conversion. Preserve settings, signing integrity and the existing signature cutoff; reject unsupported future data instead of silently interpreting it. This ticket-pack revision itself must not read, delete or reset the user's real save.

Once a new-format campaign exists, reopen it normally and retain its earned XP/badges/trust/coins/items/choices/checkpoint. Ordinary atomic writes, new-campaign backups and corruption recovery remain required. Failed initialization or saving leaves the previous committed state available, reports a retryable error and produces no success effects. New Game resets active campaign/mastery/claims/ownership but retains settings. There is one active campaign, not save slots. In-game case-file history remains a feature; removing an old-progress archive does not remove the game's document-filing workflow.

Use small pure campaign rules plus Unity adapters to existing systems. Planned resource catalogues under Resources/Campaign own reward events, trust rules, items/shop, pickups/display anchors and feature readiness. Item definitions describe ID, category, view/action adapter, unlock prerequisite, price, cap and presentation reference. State stores IDs/quantities/links. Do not build a generic RPG item scripting language.

Display target early as FUTURE community support, never an ending already won. Dismissive hold net -3; correction remains required. Do not double-count baseline case service or let multiple audits apply ethics repeatedly. ## Inventory, physical world and ownership

Bag sections: Profiles; Case Files; Tools & Supplies; Keepsakes. Include a small evidence-journal entry point backed by learned neutral story facts/record links, not a Level5 evidence minigame. Coins are wallet data, badges career data; furniture is installed, not backpack clutter. Required documents unlimited and never sell/discard. Optional default bookmarks pin three collected source items; normal viewers/comparison still work for free. Inspect anywhere during normal exploration; movement/world taps lock while viewing. Formal calculation, filing, stamping and finding submission remain workstation actions. Bag is a catalogue of actual models, not a duplicate source of case values; original and working copies remain distinct.

L1 initial pickups: the four actual profiles; Marie's actual permitted papers through original acquisition paths; one optional Coast Tales postcard keepsake with short neutral character lore and display slot; one optional appointment voucher at a clearly labeled public consultation supply point after the relevant introduction. Never give an answer-key value, essential evidence, earned badge or free permanent shop upgrade as a random pickup. Coin rewards remain activity-only. Each optional acquisition is once per campaign; reentry removes claimed pickups and does not hide a mandatory source object before its gameplay handover.

Physical supply counter near the office and the 3D Menu catalogue share ONE shop domain/state. Shop unlocked by completed Marie case; Quax introduces it before chapter wrap-up, but purchasing is optional. Opening shop/inventory cannot bypass L1 side-quest-before-office gates. Catalogue/menu viewing is paused; independently inspecting records through the bag consumes active review time, as existing independent reading does. Do not pause every record to create an unlimited hidden time-stop. Existing compulsory guide/settings/dialogue pauses stay consistent.

## Resume, launch and availability

Menu labels: New Game, Continue (chapter/task subtitle), Career, Practice, Shop, Settings. First launch Continue unavailable; Shop locked until introduced. Career can show available L1 and future chapters without exposing unsupported launches. Practice retains lesson-based unlocks. New Game has confirmation; menu return has save-before-leave, no destructive reset disguised as Continue. Intro viewing flag persists, and the current first-view skip policy is preserved; no wholesale cutscene replacement.

User menu revision (2026-10-22, supersedes the menu labels above): no started story (no committed v5 campaign; older development, newer-version and unreadable saves included) shows one PLAY note and Settings, Tutorials hidden; PLAY commits a fresh campaign and goes straight to the unskippable opening. A started story shows NEW GAME and CONTINUE side by side in PLAY's row, then Tutorials and Settings. CONTINUE opens the saved-progress module (career board) and never launches a level. NEW GAME uses the Settings board's confirmation card ("Start a New Game?"), then PLAY's route. The main menu has no Career, Shop or subtitle rows; the Shop entry lives in the progress module (top edge beside BACK) and stays locked until the shop is introduced.

L1 checkpoint covers conversations/profile IDs, placements/draft/frozen first check, quest phase/correction, objective flags, case ID/fields/transfers/form, audit history, score already earned, clock/day/deadline/extension/punctuality, actual acquisition/reward claims, follow-through/shop-intro/finale position. Save at stable domain milestones and pause/quit, plus dirty periodic updates for running clocks. Safe spawn/workstation restore; no raw camera transform, held drag, sentence offset or animation frame. Restore must not raise award/choice/pickup gameplay events. App loss after approval cannot discard completion or repeat the finale reward. Continue after A completion opens Career/next available task, never repeats L1 story.

Until T18-T21 integrate L2, mark NEW CAMPAIGN launch of L2 pending integration. Preserve its existing scene/build data for isolated development, but do not promise a coherent new-campaign continuation through legacy heart/trust paths. T18-T21 enable it together. L3-L5 remain coming-soon even if predecessor is completed. Availability and educational completion are separate.

T19 adds only the minimal authored choice needed for Rosa. Quax presentation is a scoped guide actor/identity pass, not an unrestricted art overhaul. Keep unresolved Quax art explicit in KNOWN_ISSUES and user handoff.
