## What changed

- **One canonical scale.** `ExplorationRules.ElijahRestHeight = 1.58 m`: sole to hair top in the rest pose (body ≈1.56 m), −1.6% from the cohort median. It equals the existing Level 2/3 conversation fit. `CharacterBodyGeometry.TryMeasureRest` measures the authored rest geometry (renderer transform × mesh bounds), which is independent of animation and `BakeMesh`. Exploration applies the scale **once**, on the visual parent, before the final rebind, at world scale 0.23604. Soles are grounded from the foot bones minus the measured ankle height. The idle guard compares against its own bind-time reading, so knee bends never rescale anything. The smoothed conversation Elijah uses the same calibration. The capsule, Rigidbody and NPCs are unchanged.
- **Camera (existing writer).** The follow pivot sits at hips height (0.55 H) over the soles, on a tracked floor height. The composition raises the camera along its own up axis, keeping the pivot 0.37 below the viewport centre at any pitch. Look pitch has a +12° neutral bias, limited to −8…48°. Distance is 1.91 H = **3.02 m**. The vertical FOV stays at the shared **60°**: dialogue shots, document overlays and task layouts read it, so a second FOV writer would pop at every handoff. The 45–55° start range was therefore solved at 60°.
- **Camera collision.** Hits pull the camera in at once. It holds until the way has been clear for 0.3 s, then eases out exponentially (frame-rate independent, no pumping). NPCs never zoom the view. Benches, curbs and low hedges below the rising path are passed over. Squeezed below 0.55 m, the body hides through the existing visibility owner (released above 0.75 m). The floor height holds through a hop, follows real drops and curbs smoothly, and snaps after teleports.
- **Pace and response (outdoors).** Small stick movement walks analog up to **1.45 m/s** at the rim (Elijah's walk at 1.21× its authored cadence). The outer ring runs at **3.8 m/s**. Indoor first person keeps 3.5/4.9 m/s. Acceleration is 14/s, braking 22/s, air steering 3/s with momentum kept: 75% of walk speed in 0.1 s, a run stops in ≤0.2 s, a reversal passes straight through, and release settles to exactly 0. Heading follows the wanted direction (no moonwalk) at 600°/s.
- **Animation.** Idle, walk and run blend by actual speed. Walk and run share **one** distance-driven phase over a blended stride (walk 1.354 m, run 2.29 m per cycle). Heel contacts are measured at **0.22/0.71** and authored identically into the run, so blends never jump the foot phase. Footsteps play on those contacts, plus one landing sound, never in the air. Turning in place steps the feet. The root stays fixed: one translation authority, no root motion.
- **Run clip** (`Elijah_Sprint.anim`, GUID kept). It is authored from Elijah's own imported walk: all 130 Humanoid curves, exported with provenance to `Tests/ElijahClipSource.json`. Each leg replays the walk with stance shortened from ~58% to 36% (adding a flight phase). It adds 1.55× hip flexion, restrained swing-knee flex and stance absorption. Each arm uses its own leg's timing, so opposition holds (r = −0.76). Elbows sit at ~80°, the trunk leans slightly forward with more counter-rotation, and the body is lowest at mid-stance. The cycle is 0.60 s and seamless.
- **Jump.** Physics: a 0.05 s readable windup, a 0.42 m apex (0.27 H), 0.59 s of flight, 0.10 s coyote (not after a jump), a 0.13 s buffer used once, and a 0.25 s cooldown. Walls and steep faces are not floor, and the ascending body cannot re-ground in its first 0.1 s. Airborne without a jump counts as a fall only after 0.08 s. Presentation: `ExplorationRules.JumpTrack` runs windup → rise → fall → land with monotonic progress. Rise progress follows launch speed. Fall progress comes from a bounded ground probe (time-limited without one, never playing contact early). Landing starts on genuine contact and is shorter when moving. The clip is authored as stand → crouch (0.12) → push-off → asymmetric tuck → apex (0.42) → legs reaching (0.68) → contact (0.70) → modest compression → stand (1.0), with monotone tangents.
- **Jacket.** `ElijahModelTreatment` (v4, derived copies only) now refines the outer layers only (coat, head, hair, ears). `ElijahClothingInset` places shirt vertices within 7 mm of the coat 4.7 mm beneath it: 109 of 563 move, at most 4.3 mm. The visible shirt front, collar and hands are untouched. Originals keep their bytes and GUIDs.
- **Review aids** (Editor only, `TaxVenture ▸ T03 Third-Person Review`): *Stand Beside Nearest Adult (equal depth)*, *Toggle Framing Guides* (drawn inside the camera's gameplay rect, with live feet/height/centre readout), and *Toggle Slow Motion (0.25x)*. No scene or rig assembly is needed.

## User acceptance (native; not done by AI)

1. Let Unity import (the two derived Elijah FBX re-import with treatment v4, and the clips update). Check the Console for `[ElijahModelTreatment] … inset` and `[ExplorationBody] Bound … rest 6.694 -> 1.580 m`. Start a fresh Play session in Office_Level1 and go outdoors.
2. **Scale:** *Stand Beside Nearest Adult*, then compare silhouettes at equal depth (Marie, Jhun, Milo…). Elijah should read as an ordinary adult. Then talk to an NPC: the conversation Elijah should be the same size.
3. **Framing:** *Toggle Framing Guides*. Unobstructed, the feet should sit in the yellow band, the body centred, and town and route visible above. Orbit up and down, and try a wall corner, a bench, an NPC brushing past, and a narrow path. Expect no face-sized zoom, no pumping, no clipping.
4. **Locomotion** (normal speed, then *Slow Motion*): small stick, rim walk, outer-ring run, start, stop, 90°/180° turns and turning on the spot. Look for coherent strides without skating, arms opposing legs, a slight forward lean, and footsteps on contacts.
5. **Jumps:** standing, walking and running jumps, the practice curb, a short drop off a ledge, and a press just before landing (one buffered hop; holding never repeats). Expect a continuous windup → arc → reach → soft landing, and a steady camera.
6. **Jacket:** view the coat sides and back while idle, walking, running and jumping, in both exploration and dialogue. There should be no white/angular patches and no melted look. Check the face, hair and seams.
7. **Mobile and returns:** two landscape orientations, move+look+jump with three fingers, releasing at the sprint ring, pause/focus loss, then dialogue, task/desk, indoor⇄outdoor and reload. Expect the correct view, one Elijah, the paper in hand, and no stuck input.
8. **Phone:** record the model/OS, orientation and measured frame pacing outdoors. Do not infer them.
